05 · Learning Model

Learn it. Lab it. Build it. Explain it.

The goal is not simply to recognize terminology. Students should have opportunities to apply concepts through practical work.

Lessons Learn the concepts and vocabulary.

Projects Combine multiple concepts into practical systems.
Assessment Demonstrate understanding through practical and conceptual work.

06 · Practical Work

Knowledge becomes valuable when you can demonstrate it.

Project-based learning can connect individual lessons into something students can actually explain, document and improve.

Systems Project

Build a Linux Environment

Work with operating-system concepts, users, permissions, services, processes, storage and troubleshooting.

Cloud Project

Design Infrastructure

Translate an application requirement into networking, compute, storage, security and automation components.

Kubernetes Project

Deploy a Workload

Explore containers, workloads, services, cluster concepts, deployment and operational visibility.

SRE Project

Observe a System

Use reliability concepts, metrics, logs and system health signals to understand operational behavior.

Security Project

Protect Infrastructure

Explore identity, permissions, secrets, network boundaries, hardening and infrastructure security principles.

Architecture Project

Design a Platform

Bring systems, cloud, networking, automation, security and reliability together into an architecture.

13 · Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before you begin.

Do I need previous technology experience?

No. The Foundations pathway is designed to introduce the concepts needed to understand infrastructure. More advanced courses may have prerequisites.

Do I need a degree?

A degree is not presented as a prerequisite for beginning the learning pathway. Individual advanced programs may define their own recommended background.

Do I need to know programming?

Programming experience can be useful, but infrastructure includes many concepts beyond application programming. Entry requirements will be stated for each course.

Are the courses self-paced or live?

Delivery formats will be identified for each cohort. The program may combine lessons, practical work, projects and assessment.

Will there be hands-on labs?

Hands-on learning is part of the educational model. Specific lab environments and requirements will be published with each course.

Will I receive a certificate?

Applicable programs may provide a certificate of completion after the defined requirements are met. External accreditation or credentialing will only be represented when formally established.

Is Inside Infrastructure accredited?

Accreditation should only be claimed after a formal accreditation relationship or recognized status has actually been established. Partnership and credentialing opportunities are being developed separately from the curriculum itself.

What is Daemon Academy?

Daemon Academy is the youth education program for students ages 11–17, with age-appropriate learning around technology, AI, networks, cybersecurity, infrastructure and building.

When will pricing be available?

Winter and Spring pricing will be published once tuition, schedules, course structure and enrollment policies are finalized.

Can organizations partner with Inside Infrastructure?

Yes. The platform is being designed to support potential education, technology, workforce, youth, community, project and future credentialing partnerships.
